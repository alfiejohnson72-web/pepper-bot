# FAQ (the bot only answers from this file and catalogue.json, so keep it up to date)

## Delivery
- UK only for now.
- Orders placed before 2pm (Mon to Fri) are sent the same day by Royal Mail Tracked 48, usually arriving in 2 to 3 working days.
- Delivery is £3.95, or free on orders over £50 and on every subscription refill.
- You get a tracking email once your order is sent.

## Payment
- Checkout is a secure Stripe page. You can pay by card, Apple Pay or Google Pay.
- We never ask for card details in Telegram.

## Subscriptions ("Refill Club")
- A new bottle every 6 weeks, about 15% off, with free delivery.
- Cancel any time, no minimum term. Use the "manage subscription" link in your emails, or tap "Talk to a human" and we'll sort it.

## Returns
- Unopened products can be returned within 14 days of delivery for a full refund.
- For hygiene reasons we can't take back opened products, unless they arrived damaged or faulty.
- For a damaged or wrong item, message the team (tap "Talk to a human") with a photo within 7 days.

## Safety
- All products are cosmetics, for use on the skin only, and safety assessed for the UK.
- Patch test on your inner arm 24 hours before first use.
- If you're pregnant, breastfeeding, or have a skin condition or allergy, check the ingredients and speak to your GP or pharmacist first.
- Stop using it if irritation happens.

## Results
- Most people notice skin feels smoother within 2 to 4 weeks. Visible changes in fine lines usually take 8 to 12 weeks of daily use.
- Results vary from person to person.

## Contact
- Tap "Talk to a human" in the bot, or type /help. The team replies within 24 hours, usually much faster.
