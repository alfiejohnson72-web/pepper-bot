# FAQ (this is only the starting text: after launch, edit the Q&A in the admin dashboard under Settings)

## Delivery
- UK only for now.
- Orders placed before 2pm (Mon to Fri) are usually sent the same day by Royal Mail Tracked 48, arriving in 2 to 3 working days.
- Delivery is £3.95, or free on orders over £50.
- You get a Telegram message with tracking once your order is sent. Tap "Track an order" any time.

## Payment
- Checkout is a secure Stripe page. You can pay by card, Apple Pay or Google Pay.
- We never ask for card details in Telegram.

## Returns
- Unopened products can be returned within 14 days of delivery for a full refund.
- For hygiene reasons we can't take back opened products, unless they arrived damaged or faulty.
- For a damaged or wrong item, tap "Contact support" and send a photo within 7 days.

## Using SPF
- Apply generously as the last step of your morning routine.
- Reapply every 2 hours in the sun, and after swimming, sweating or towelling.
- No sunscreen gives 100% protection. Use shade and clothing too in strong sun.

## Safety
- All products are cosmetics, for use on the skin only, and safety assessed for the UK.
- Patch test on your inner arm 24 hours before first use.
- If you're pregnant, breastfeeding, or have a skin condition or allergy, check the ingredients with your GP or pharmacist first.
- Stop using it if irritation happens.

## Contact
- Tap "Contact support" in the menu, or type /help. The team replies within 24 hours, usually much faster.
